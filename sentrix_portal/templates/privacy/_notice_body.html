What never leaves your home

Face photos and their embeddings, evidence images and continuous video stay on the device. When you watch remotely the video passes through the SENTRIX server in memory over an encrypted connection; it is not end-to-end encrypted and it is never stored.

Where it is kept, and for how long

Stored in India; messages transit Telegram and the e-mail provider. SOS snapshots are deleted after {{ caps.sos_snapshot_days }} days, incident records after {{ caps.unreviewed_days }} days (true alarms after {{ caps.true_days }} days), the audit log after {{ caps.audit_days }} days. Your rights

You can see, export and correct your data, withdraw any optional consent as easily as you gave it (Privacy page), and close the account. Minors never get an account and their faces are never enrolled.
