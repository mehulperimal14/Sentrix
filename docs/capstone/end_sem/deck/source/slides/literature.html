Each of us surveyed eight papers in our own area, forty in all, ten of them from 2024 to 2026; the full table is in the report, and every reference was checked against its source. The pattern: strong components exist, but they are evaluated per frame on curated datasets, and none of them answers who gets alerted or how a household experiences false alarms.
