02 · Scope and utility

One room, three threats, the owner in control

In scope

  • Replica handgun and visible flame (alarm classes)
  • Fights confirmed by video and aligned audio
  • Faces per tracked person, body re-identification
  • Five alarm levels with encrypted evidence
  • Owner-approved SOS: Portal, device screen, Telegram, e-mail
  • Raspberry Pi 5 unit in a 3D-printed case; training recordings

Out of scope

  • Night vision (needs an infrared camera and data)
  • Calling 112 automatically: SENTRIX never does
  • Outdoor and multi-camera perimeters
  • A trained fusion model: it needs labelled alarms from real homes first

For the household

Alarms worth trusting, control over who is alerted, and video, faces and evidence that never leave home.

For responders

A dispatch package with the address, entry notes, household details, a snapshot and the timeline.

Scope is deliberately narrow: one indoor room, three threats where minutes matter. Robbery, stick and smoke are logged but never go above Level 2. We never dial 112 ourselves; the owner decides, with safety defaults for when they can’t. The unit itself is a Raspberry Pi 5 with Camera Module 3, a USB microphone and three status LEDs, in our own 3D-printed case.
