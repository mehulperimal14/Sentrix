Three model generations on our labelled footage. The custom DINOv2 classifier nearly doubled the fine-tuned model’s macro-F1, from 0.401 to 0.768, and cut its false alarms nine-fold; the evidence rules then removed the rest without losing a detected episode. In November we recorded new scenes and retrained: held-out AUROC rose to 0.87 for weapons and 0.96 for fire, with no false alarms added. We switched the Pi to the new fire head on 21 November, but in that night’s live tests it did not yet recognise the demo room’s flames, so flame recordings from that room come next. Slim CNNs for the Pi failed a bar fixed in advance, so DINOv2 stayed.
