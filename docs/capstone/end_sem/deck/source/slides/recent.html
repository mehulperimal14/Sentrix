04 · Literature survey (2) · Recent work and research gaps

What 2024–2026 changed, and the gaps SENTRIX fills

2025 · Weapon review

101 papers report 78–99.5% precision but little real-world testing [25].

Gap: judged per frame, not per alarm

SENTRIX: episodes and false alarms per hour, per level

2024–2026 · Domain shift

Re-ID surveys centre on new cameras and settings [26, 27].

Gap: test sets share the training rooms

SENTRIX: splits by video; per-room calibration

2024–2025 · Sound + video

Aligning audio before fusing reaches 86.07% AP [28, 29].

Gap: fusion without time alignment

SENTRIX: audio votes only when aligned

2024–2026 · Policy

Seattle police want evidence before dispatch [2]; DPDP Rules notified [12].

Gap: alerts with no owner in the loop

SENTRIX: answer window, PINs, safety defaults

2024–2026 · Edge

NMS-free YOLO cuts edge latency [35, 76]; Pi AI HAT+ 2 has 40 TOPS [19].

Gap: single models timed, not a full stack

SENTRIX: the whole stack measured on a Pi 5

2024–2025 · VLMs, LLMs

LLM-based detectors still fall short on home video [30, 80].

Gap: private video analysed in the cloud

SENTRIX: all inference on the device

[n]: reference numbers of the report (Appendix A).

The panel asked what is new: six recent developments, each with its gap and our answer. A 2025 review of 101 weapon papers finds high precision but little real-world testing, so we judge SENTRIX by alarms per hour, not per frame. Re-identification surveys centre on domain shift, the problem we met in a new room, so we split by video and calibrate per room. Audio-visual work shows that alignment matters, so our sound votes only when it overlaps the frame. Police want evidence first, so the owner decides and every request carries a snapshot. Pi accelerators improved, and we measured the whole stack on a Pi. And language-model detectors still fall short at home and would take video out of the house, so everything runs on the device.
