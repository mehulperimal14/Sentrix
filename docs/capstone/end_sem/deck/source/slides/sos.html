08 · How it works

The owner decides who is alerted

1 · Confirmed Level 3

Weapon, fire or a corroborated fight

2 · Owner asked

Portal, device screen, Telegram, e-mail

3 · Answer

Within 30, 45 or 60 minutes

4 · Desk

Simulated monitoring centre: acknowledges, closes, records the verdict

Help

Contacts and the SENTRIX Desk get the dispatch: address, entry notes, snapshot, timeline

False alarm

Needs the SOS PIN; five wrong PINs lock answers for 10 minutes

No answer

Recorded as “unanswered”, never “safe”; “Request help now” stays open

Safety defaults and duress

Contacts are alerted anyway for a corroborated Level 4/5 (at once), a confirmed fire (after 2 minutes) or a device that goes offline during an alarm. A duress PIN looks like a false alarm on screen but silently alerts the contacts.

SENTRIX never calls 112 itself. A confirmed Level 3 opens one request, and the owner is asked everywhere they might be: the Portal, the device screen, Telegram and e-mail. Approve sends the dispatch; a false alarm needs a PIN so an intruder cannot cancel it; silence is recorded as unanswered, never as safe. Safety defaults cover the owner who cannot answer, and the duress PIN covers the owner who is being forced. Every one of these is a tested state transition.
