The gap: smart cameras send private video to the cloud and stop when the Internet does; cloud face recognition in doorbells drew privacy objections in Dec. 2025, and India’s DPDP Rules (Nov. 2025) now phase in consent duties. The first minutes of an intrusion, a fire or a fight still fall on the people inside.

Three facts frame the problem. Almost all alarm activations are false, and police are reacting: since October 2024 Seattle responds only to alarms backed by audio, video or a witness. Burglary is common in India: NCRB’s 2023 report, released in September 2025, counts over 1.07 lakh cases, 83% at night. Cloud cameras add a privacy problem, as the December 2025 objections to cloud face recognition in doorbells showed, and India’s DPDP Rules notified in November 2025 make consent and erasure obligations concrete.
