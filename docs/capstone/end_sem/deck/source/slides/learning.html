10 · Professional and technical learning

What building the whole product taught us

I

Technical

  • Measure what the user experiences: alarm levels, not frames
  • Self-supervised backbones transfer; small CNNs did not
  • Time alignment and alert state machines are testable logic
  • Edge limits: threads, runtime spin-waits, Pi power and GPIO
  • Secure web: Argon2id, TOTP, CSP, CSRF, outbound-only links

II

Professional

  • Contract first: eight parallel tracks merged in days
  • Fix the acceptance bar before running the experiment
  • Report limits next to results; correct earlier claims
  • Consent, privacy, and never auto-calling 112
  • Reviews where every finding needs a failing case
The biggest technical lesson was that per-frame accuracy hid an unusable product; measuring committed alarm levels changed what we built. Professionally, writing the Portal–device contract first let us split into eight tracks and integrate quickly, and fixing acceptance bars in advance kept us honest, including correcting claims from our own mid-semester report.
