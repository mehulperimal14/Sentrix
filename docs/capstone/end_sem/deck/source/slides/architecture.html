Read left to right. Camera Module 3 and the USB microphone feed one Python process on the Pi, in four stages: perception produces votes, the evidence rules confirm them, the TCI and level policy commit a level, and an incident with encrypted evidence follows, with an SOS request for weapon or fire. Three LEDs show running, attention or alarm. The device opens only an outbound, encrypted link to the Portal, always on at Render, which asks the owner and then alerts the contacts and the Desk.
