# Every dataset labelled, every model evaluated (2026-11-18/19)

The owner's request: label everything (the five new videos, the old custom set,
the public corpora), measure every model, then fine-tune, calibrate and
install. Every number below comes from a report in `training/runs/` (path in
each section). "Held out" means data no model being compared was trained or
tuned on: the custom **val** videos (`gun`, `vid04`) and the **test** video
`wa02` (reported only, never used to choose anything). The five new videos are
in the **train** split (the owner held none out), so they are unseen by the v4
models and in-sample for the v5 ones.

### The whole system: replay of all 15 videos (`custom_eval/baseline_2026-11-19/`, `v5det_2026-11-19/`, `v5all_2026-11-19/`)

Tick mode, every video's own soundtrack, committed levels; `wa02` replayed for
reporting only.

| | v4 models | v5 detector heads + v4 scene (the default now) | all three v5 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Frame accuracy / macro-F1 | 0.790 / 0.519 | 0.798 / 0.524 | **0.901 / 0.729** |
| Episodes detected | 13 / 22 | 15 / 22 | **16 / 22** |
| False L3 onsets | 1 | 1 | **0** |
| Weapon episodes at L3 / L4 / L5 | 9 / 6 / 2 of 14 | 11 / 6 / 2 | 11 / 8 / 2 |
| Fire episodes at L3 / L4 / L5 | 3 / 2 / 2 of 3 | 3 / 2 / **0** | 3 / 2 / **0** |
| New gun and knife scenes | L2 (missed) | L3 | L3 |

The fire L5 drop is a regression on in-sample fire footage: the v5 fire head is
more conservative than v4, which had memorised those frames (train AUROC 1.0).
Assault stays 0 / 3 everywhere (the staged fights are visually ambiguous and
have no aggression sound). Robbery is experimental and stays at L2 on its own
(D1): its evidence never exceeds TCI 0.35. The one robbery episode "caught at
L3" with the v5 heads (`mmtheft`) was lifted there by a **false weapon box**:
the v5 weapon head called the dark bag the masked intruder holds a "pistol"
(0.67, t = 34.7 s). Robbery frames are excluded from detector training (they
might show a weapon), so the head never learned that this bag is not one; the
fix for the next retrain is to mark weapon-free robbery videos as negatives.
It fired on no held-out normal frame.

### The Pi's configuration: clip mode, ONNX at 320 px, fast lane, v4 scene (`custom_eval/pi_clip_v4heads_2026-11-19/`, `pi_clip_v5heads_2026-11-19/`)

The 14 train + val videos (`wa02` not replayed), the same settings as

|---|---|---|
| False L3 onsets | 0 | 0 |
| Weapon episodes at L3 / L4 / L5 (of 11) | 9 / 5 / 4 | 9 / 4 / 3 |
| Fire episodes at L3 / L4 / L5 (of 3) | 3 / 2 / 1 | 3 / **0 / 0** |

| Held-out val episodes (gun, vid04) | weapon L3, fire L3 | weapon L3, fire L3 (identical) |

At 320 px the tick-mode gain disappears: L3 is unchanged, and the v5 fire head
no longer corroborates fire to L4/L5 on the training fire videos the v4 head
had memorised (held-out vid04 reaches L3 only with either). The v5 heads were
trained at 640 px; a 320 px fine-tune for the Pi's object stage, and flame
footage from the demo room, are the next fire steps. Keeping v4 fire on the Pi
(`SENTRIX_FIRE_WEIGHTS=models/v4_custom/fire_detector_custom_v4.pt` with its
`_320.onnx`) would restore those L4s at no L3 cost; the owner decides.
